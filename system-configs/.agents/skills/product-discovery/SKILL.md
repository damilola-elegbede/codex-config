## Checklist
- Define target personas, JTBD, and desired outcomes.
- Collect existing research, support tickets, and product analytics.
- Explore solution hypotheses with risks, assumptions, and validation paths.
- Shape experiments or prototypes with acceptance signals.
- Align roadmap decisions with engineering capacity and sequencing.

## Prompts
- "What risks must we retire before committing to full delivery?"
- "Outline a discovery cadence that integrates build/measure/learn loops."
