## Checklist
- Define release scope, owners, and success metrics.
- Confirm environments, feature flags, and migration steps are staged.
- Align QA, docs, and support readiness checklists.
- Plan comms timelines (pre-release, launch, post-release) with stakeholders.
- Validate rollback and hotfix paths are documented and tested.

## Prompts
- "What cues tell us to hold the release and who makes the call?"
- "Draft the status page or customer email template in case of issues."
