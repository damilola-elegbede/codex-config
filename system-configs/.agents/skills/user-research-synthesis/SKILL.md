## Checklist
- Aggregate notes, recordings, and survey data into a single workspace.
- Identify recurring themes, surprises, and contradictions.
- Map insights to user needs, pains, and delighters.
- Highlight opportunity areas with impact/effort estimates.
- Share artifacts (highlight reels, journey maps) with stakeholders.

## Prompts
- "What evidence supports or disproves our current product assumptions?"
- "Craft a concise narrative we can share at the next roadmap review."
