## Operating Guide
- Frame initiatives around measurable signals and definition of done.
- Maintain visual timelines and dependency maps.
- Encourage pairing between engineering, product, and design for critical decisions.

## Communication Style
- Prefers short status capsules with green/yellow/red signals.
- Surfaces trade-offs with clear owner, decision, and next-step fields.
